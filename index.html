Struggling mid-stretch?

Sips that never break the rhythm

Still or sparkling water (add a pinch of natural salt, or magnesium), plain tea or coffee, sugar-free pickle juice or olive brine, and water flavored with lemon, lime, or ginger. Hydration + salt quietly fixes most "hunger," headaches, and fog — especially in the first 48 hours.

Training wheels — rescue a wobbling rebuilding day

Up to 3 teaspoons of heavy cream, coconut cream, or full-fat milk in tea or coffee · butter, ghee, or MCT oil (a spoonful of butter with a pinch of salt counts) · bone broth or low-carb vegetable broth, eaten from a bowl with a spoon. These barely move insulin, so 16 wobbly hours can still become a finished stretch. Wean off them as rebuilding gets easier — they're comfort, not fuel for the long run.

Not a training wheel

Anything with real protein or carbs — a bite of steak, eggs, cheese, a cracker — is food, and food means the day has become a nourishing day (or a fat-fasting day, if you stick to high-fat foods to satiety). No verdict attached: if you're hungry enough to want ribeye, that's your body asking to eat, not a craving to outwit.

Stop and nourish now

The rule: stop anytime you feel unwell or uncertain — uncertain alone is enough. Stop immediately and check with your doctor for: nausea · flu-like symptoms · a measured blood-glucose drop that worries you. Mild headache, dizziness, or fog is often just low sodium — try water with a pinch of salt or pickle juice — but if that doesn't settle it, eat. A positive pregnancy test means fasting stops entirely, that day. You can always fast another day.

Adjusting the plan

Ended a rebuilding day early

The hours still counted. Pick up at your next planned meal and leave the rest of the week untouched — no make-up stretches.

A stretch ran long

Enjoy it — and keep the plan as it was. Plans built to match your best days stop fitting your ordinary ones.

Hungrier days are real days

Period days, bad sleep, stress, travel — appetite genuinely shifts with them, especially in the week before your period. Shrinking a stretch on those days is working with your hormones, not against your plan. And a day that accidentally became a long stretch because you were out? It happened; the week doesn't owe anyone symmetry.

While trying to conceive

Shorter plans are the safer companions: every cycle has a window where you wouldn't yet know. A positive test ends fasting entirely, that day.

Progress, not perfection.
