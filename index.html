3 Your week

Life happened? Tap any meal to switch it on or off for that day — struck-through means your body keeps rebuilding through it. The rest of the week stays put, the way the book advises.

Ended a rebuilding day early? The hours still counted — pick up at your next planned meal, no make-up stretches. Rebuilding ran long because it felt easy? Enjoy it, but don't let the plan inflate to match your best days. While trying to conceive, the shorter plans remain the safer companions.

Progress, not perfection.
